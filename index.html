Jayagoo shopping guide

A shortlist is only as good as its details.

When researching Jayagoo finds, use the same checks for every candidate. A consistent record makes differences easier to spot and prevents a striking image from replacing useful information.

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Decide whether you need an everyday shoe, a warm layer or an accessory. Start with the relevant category instead of comparing unrelated products.

  2. Read the current listing

    Record the product link, selected variant, seller, currency and available measurements. Save notes about any information the listing does not provide.

  3. Compare the full purchase cost

    Account for the item price, delivery, applicable service fees, currency conversion and any taxes or import charges relevant to your destination.

  4. Resolve uncertainties before ordering

    Confirm stock, return terms, material details and seller credentials. Treat photos as visual evidence of appearance, not proof of authenticity.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, Jayagoo spreadsheet refers to the search topic of organized fashion product lists. A useful comparison list records product links, categories, measurements, listed prices and research notes. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the links on this page go?

All navigation links lead to CAFANS. Category links open the corresponding CAFANS category, while general browsing links open the CAFANS homepage.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. A photo, logo or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s identity, provenance, purchase documentation and return terms, and use an authorized retailer when authenticity is essential.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item that already fits. For clothing, check chest width, length, shoulders and waist. For shoes, compare the stated sizing system and available foot-length or insole measurements.

Are prices, stock and shipping costs verified here?

No. This page does not provide live inventory, verified prices or shipping quotes. Confirm availability, currency, delivery options, fees and return terms on the current listing before purchasing.
